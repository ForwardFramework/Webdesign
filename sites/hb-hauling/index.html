Questions

Straight answers

How much does it cost?

It depends on volume, weight and access — a few bags out of a garage isn't a packed basement. Text a photo or two to 412-992-8796 and you'll get a free quote, usually without anyone needing to come out first.

Do I have to move anything to the curb?

No. We do the lifting and carrying from wherever it sits — basement, attic, back yard, behind the shed. That's the whole point of calling us.

Can you handle a full house or estate cleanout?

Yes. Estates, evictions, rentals between tenants and hoarding situations included. We'll walk it, price it, and clear it room by room.

Do you do the repair work too, or only hauling?

Both. We handle demo, drywall, general repairs and handyman work alongside the hauling — so the wall that comes out can get patched back up without you calling a second company.

How fast can you get here?

Depends on the day and the size of the job. Storm damage and blocked driveways get moved up the list. Call or text and we'll give you a real window, not a guess.

What do you take?

Furniture, appliances, mattresses, scrap metal, construction debris, yard waste, brush, trees and general junk. Hazardous material (chemicals, fuel, asbestos) we can't take — ask when you call and we'll point you the right way.
