Questions

Straight answers

Can you come out today?

Most days, yes — same-day service is what we're known for, and we handle emergency no-heat and no-cool calls around the clock. Call 412-913-5291 and we'll tell you honestly what today looks like instead of stringing you along.

Do you charge for a diagnostic?

There's a standard service call fee for coming out and diagnosing the problem, quoted to you before we schedule. If you move forward with the repair, we tell you exactly what it costs up front — flat rate, approved by you before any work starts.

Another company says I need a whole new system. Should I believe them?

Get a second opinion first — ours is free. Bring us the written estimate and we'll inspect the system ourselves. Sometimes a replacement really is the right call, and we'll say so. Plenty of times it's a repair, and customers have told us they were quoted a full unit for a part that needed replaced.

Do you work on older Pittsburgh homes?

Constantly. Century-old South Hills houses, converted boilers, tight basements, odd ductwork — it's most of what we see. We size equipment to the house you actually have.

How much does a new furnace or AC cost?

It depends on the size of your home, the efficiency you want, and what's already in place. We give a written, itemized quote after an in-home assessment — free, with no pressure. Ask about financing options when we're out.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — fully licensed and insured for residential HVAC work in Pennsylvania. Happy to provide documentation before we start.

Do you do water heaters too?

We do. Tank and tankless installation and replacement, including hauling away the old unit.
