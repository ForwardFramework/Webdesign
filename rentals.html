Before you book

The rules, in plain English

Our carts are street legal, which means they follow the same rules as a car. Nothing here is a surprise on delivery day.

  • Licensed drivers 18+. Every driver needs a valid licence and must be named on the rental agreement.
  • Seat belts for everyone. One passenger per belted seat — no riding on laps or standing.
  • Posted roads only. Street-legal carts stay off the beach, the sand and the sidewalks.
  • Charge it overnight. Electric carts plug into a standard outlet at your rental; gas carts go back with the fuel they arrived with.
  • Questions are welcome. We walk you through everything at delivery and stay a phone call away.
