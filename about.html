Our story

Built on repeat clients and word of mouth

Yasse started this company with a simple idea: send the same trusted people to the same homes, quote a flat price, and stand behind the work. That’s it. No upsells at the door, no surprise line items, no different crew every month.

It turns out that’s rare enough to build a business on. Most of our schedule now comes from clients who’ve been with us for years and the neighbors they sent our way — the kind of growth that only happens when people are genuinely happy to open the door.

We clean homes across Lakewood Ranch, Bradenton and Sarasota: family houses, seasonal residences, condos, and a growing list of vacation rentals. Every one of them runs off the same written checklist and the same guarantee.

What we stand on

Four things we don’t compromise on

The same team

You get the same cleaners visit after visit. They learn your home, your preferences and where things go — and you stop having to explain it.

Trust, verified

Every cleaner is background-checked and personally trained by us, and we carry general liability insurance. Certificate available for your HOA or manager.

Safe products

Eco-friendly, low-odor products that are safe around kids and pets by default. Prefer we use yours? Leave them out and say the word.

Honest pricing

Flat rates published on the website. The quote is the price. No fuel surcharges, no “it was worse than expected” conversations.

Our people

Hired carefully, trained properly, paid fairly

Cleaning is skilled work, and the difference between an average clean and a great one is entirely the person doing it.

Screened

Background check, reference check and an in-person interview before anyone sets foot in a client’s home.

Trained

Every cleaner works our written checklist alongside an experienced team member before they run a home on their own.

Kept

We pay above the local average and build stable routes, because a team that stays is the only way to send the same faces every visit.
