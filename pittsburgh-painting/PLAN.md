# Pittsburgh Painting & Property Solutions — Website Strategy & Build Plan

**Client:** Pittsburgh Painting & Property Solutions
**Contact:** Brian G — (412) 537-4866
**Socials:** FB `Pittsburgh Painting and Property Solutions` · IG `@pittsburgh_paint_goat`
**Primary goal:** turn traffic (Google, Google Business Profile, Facebook/Instagram, yard signs, referrals) into booked in-home estimates.
**Single success metric:** *estimate requests per 100 visitors.* Everything below is subordinate to that number.

---

## 1. Who is actually landing on this site

| Segment | Share (typical for a local painter) | What they need before they call |
|---|---|---|
| **Ready-now homeowner** — got a referral or saw the truck, wants a price | ~25% | Phone number, proof you're insured, fast reply promise |
| **Comparison shopper** — has 2–3 quotes already | ~45% | Before/afters, reviews, what's *included* vs. the cheap bid, warranty |
| **Researcher** — "how much does it cost to paint a house in Pittsburgh" | ~25% | Price ranges, process, prep detail; a low-commitment way to stay in touch |
| **Property manager / investor / flipper** | ~5% | Scope breadth, turnaround speed, repeat-volume terms |

**Design consequence:** the ready-now buyer gets a sticky phone button on every screen. The comparison shopper gets the before/after slider and the offer stack. The researcher gets price ranges and a downloadable checklist (email capture) so they don't leave empty. The investor gets a dedicated section on the Renovations page.

## 2. The offer

A "free estimate" is not an offer — every competitor says it. The offer has to reduce *risk* and *effort*, not just price.

### The headline promotion (seasonal, swappable)

> ### $1,000 OFF full exterior repaints booked for Aug · Sept · Oct
> Plus a free color rendering of your actual house before you commit a dollar.

This mirrors the flyer already in market, so paid ads, yard signs and the site all say the same thing — message match is worth more than clever copy.

### The risk-reversal stack — "The Paint Goat Promise"

Presented as six line items with dollar values, because a stack that *looks* like a stack converts better than a paragraph:

| # | Promise | Why it converts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Free color consultation + digital rendering of your home** (a $350 value) | Removes the #1 stall: "I don't know what color to pick." Also a legitimate reason to book *now*. |
| 2 | **Written, itemized quote in 24 hours** — line by line, no lump sums | Comparison shoppers can finally compare apples to apples; positions the cheap bid as vague. |
| 3 | **Sherwin-Williams products, two full coats — standard, never an upsell** | Named brand = borrowed credibility. "Two coats standard" directly attacks the low bidder. |
| 4 | **Written prep spec** — wash, scrape, sand, prime bare wood, caulk, mask | Prep is where cheap jobs fail. Publishing the spec is the single strongest differentiator a painter has. |
| 5 | **Daily cleanup, protected landscaping, same crew start to finish** | Answers the unspoken fear: strangers wrecking my house for two weeks. |
| 6 | **Written workmanship warranty** — peel, blister or crack and we come back free | Converts the fence-sitter. Terms must be confirmed by the owner (see §9). |

### Secondary conversion (for the ~60% who will never fill out a form today)

**Lead magnet:** *"The Pittsburgh Homeowner's Exterior Paint Checklist — 12 things Western PA weather does to a paint job, and how to spec around them."*
Email-only capture, one field. Feeds a nurture sequence and a spring re-marketing list. Regionally specific = far higher opt-in than a generic guide.

### Urgency that isn't fake

Real scarcity only: the calendar. "Aug–Oct exterior slots" and "we book roughly N exteriors a month" are true constraints. **Do not** ship a fake evergreen countdown timer — comparison shoppers notice, and it poisons the trust the rest of the page is building.

```

/estimate.html                 Free Estimate — multi-step form (the conversion engine)
/offer.html                    $1,000 Off landing page — paid traffic only, no nav
/gallery.html                  Before & After — the highest-intent proof page
/reviews.html                  Reviews & testimonials
/about.html                    About Brian + the crew + credentials
/service-areas.html            Local SEO hub — neighborhoods & suburbs
/services/exterior-painting.html
/services/interior-painting.html
/services/cabinet-refinishing.html
/services/renovations.html     Full renos, flips, kitchens, baths, flooring, basements
/thank-you.html                Conversion confirmation + tracking fire + referral ask
/privacy.html                  Required for Google Ads, Meta lead forms, SMS consent
/404.html
```

### Why this shape

- **One page per service** because Google ranks pages, not businesses. "Cabinet refinishing Pittsburgh" and "exterior painting Pittsburgh" are different searches with different buyers and different price points; one blended "Services" page ranks for neither.
- **A dedicated estimate page** so ads and every CTA on the site have one clean destination with tracking on it.
- **A separate offer landing page** with the nav stripped out, because paid traffic converts materially better without an escape hatch in the header.
- **A service-areas hub** so the site has a legitimate answer for "painters near me" in Mt. Lebanon, Sewickley, Cranberry, etc. without spinning up thin doorway pages.

## 4. What each page has to accomplish

### Home — *"Are these people legit, and are they for me?"*

| # | Section | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sticky header | Phone always one tap away; "Free Estimate" always visible |
| 2 | Hero | Answer *what, where, and what's in it for me* in under 2 seconds. Dual CTA: call + estimate |
| 3 | Trust strip | Insured · family owned & operated · Sherwin-Williams · written warranty |
| 4 | Promo band | The $1,000 off offer, above the fold on mobile |
| 5 | Stats | Count-up: homes painted, years, review average, warranty length |
| 6 | Services grid | Route the visitor to their intent in one click |
| 7 | **Before / After slider** | The most persuasive element on the site. Interactive, draggable |
| 8 | How it works — 4 steps | Kill process anxiety: what happens after I hit send |
| 9 | The Paint Goat Promise | The offer stack (§2) |
| 10 | Reviews | Social proof next to a CTA, not stranded on its own page |
| 11 | Service areas | "They work in my neighborhood" |
| 12 | FAQ accordion | Handle price, timeline, insurance, weather objections |
| 13 | Final CTA + short form | Catch the scroller who's now sold |
| 14 | Footer | NAP, hours, socials, insurance, sitemap |
| — | Mobile sticky bar | Call · Text · Estimate, thumb-height, every screen |

### Service pages — *"Do they actually know how to do MY job?"*
Hero with the specific service → what's included (checklist) → **the prep/process spec in detail** → materials → before/after slice filtered to that service → honest price ranges → service-specific FAQ → CTA. The prep spec is what separates a $6,000 bid from an $11,000 bid; publishing it is how you justify being the higher one.

### Gallery — *"Show me, don't tell me."*
Filterable (Exterior / Interior / Cabinets / Renovations / Basements). Every project gets a location, a scope line, and where possible a real before/after pair. Highest-value page on the site for comparison shoppers.

### About — *"Who is coming to my house?"*
Brian's face and story, the crew, insurance and licensing, the local angle. People hire people. Trades sites that hide the owner convert worse.

### Estimate — *"How much friction stands between me and a price?"*
Four short steps instead of one long form: project type → property details → timeline → contact. Progress bar visible. Phone number captured at the *last* step, after commitment is built. Expect materially higher completion than a single 8-field form.

### Offer landing page — *paid traffic only.*
No nav, no footer links, one CTA repeated. Message-matched to the ad creative dollar for dollar.

### Thank you — *don't waste the highest-trust moment on the site.*
Confirm what happens next and when → fire the conversion event for Google/Meta → ask for a follow on Instagram → offer the referral bonus.

## 5. Conversion mechanics baked into the build

Sourced from current home-services CRO practice (see §10):

- **Phone number in the header on every page**, tap-to-call on mobile, tracked as a conversion.
- **CTA above the fold and repeated roughly every 1.5 screens** — a visitor decides at an unpredictable scroll depth.
- **Multi-step form** — asks the easy question first; each answer given raises the odds of finishing.
- **Minimum viable fields.** Name, phone, address, project type. Everything else is asked on the phone.
- **Social proof adjacent to every major CTA**, never quarantined on a reviews page.
- **Real photography over stock, always.** Stock photos of strangers painting a stranger's house actively lose bids in this trade.
- **Speed as a conversion feature** — no framework, no jQuery, no icon font, no carousel library. Target LCP under 1.5s on 4G.
- **Thumb-first mobile.** Sticky bottom action bar; every tap target ≥ 44×44px.
- **Trust signals in the footer** — insurance, family-owned, service radius.

## 6. Motion & micro-interaction spec

Premium feel comes from restraint. Rules for this build:

- **Only `transform` and `opacity` animate.** Never width, height, top or left — those trigger layout and drop frames on mid-range Android, which is most of this audience.
- **Scroll reveal:** 12px rise + fade, 500ms, `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, staggered 60ms within a group. Fires once via `IntersectionObserver`, then unobserves.
- **Paint-swipe heading underline:** a gold bar wipes in under section headings via `transform: scaleX()` — brand-appropriate, costs one composited layer.
- **Buttons:** 2px lift + shadow bloom on hover, press-down on `:active`. 160ms. Gives physical feedback.
- **Cards:** 4px lift, border warms to gold. 200ms.
- **Before/after slider:** pointer-drag + arrow-key support, clip-path driven, no library.
- **Stat count-up:** runs once on entry, ~1.2s ease-out.
- **Accordions:** `grid-template-rows: 0fr → 1fr` — animates to natural height with no JS height measurement.
- **Header:** condenses and gains a shadow past 40px scroll, throttled with `requestAnimationFrame`.
- **Mobile nav:** slide-in panel with focus trap and `Esc` to close.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` kills all of it** and shows final states immediately. Non-negotiable — this is an accessibility requirement, not a preference.

Total JS budget: one deferred file, no dependencies.

## 7. Technical build

| Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build step | Fastest possible LCP; anyone can edit it; deploys free to Netlify/Vercel/Cloudflare Pages; nothing to break at 2am |
| Design tokens as CSS custom properties | Colors, spacing, type scale in one place at the top of `site.css` |
| Two Google fonts, four weights total | Barlow Condensed (display, matches the logo's condensed lockup) + Inter (UI/body) |
| `LocalBusiness` + `Service` + `FAQPage` JSON-LD | Rich results and Google Business Profile reinforcement |
| SVG logo, re-drawn from the raster original | Sharp at every size, ~4KB instead of ~200KB |
| Lazy loading + explicit width/height on every image | Cumulative Layout Shift stays near zero |

### Brand tokens

```
Gold        #F7B32B   primary — buttons, accents, the paint swipe
Gold light  #FFC845   hovers, highlights
Gold deep   #A66A00   gold-on-white TEXT only (the bright gold fails contrast on white)
Ink         #0E1116   headings, footer, dark sections
Slate       #5B6673   body copy
Paper       #FFFFFF / #F6F7F9   backgrounds
```

**Accessibility rule that matters here:** `#F7B32B` on white is roughly 1.9:1 — it must never carry text. Bright gold is for fills and rules; `#A66A00` carries any gold-toned text on light backgrounds.

## 8. Measurement

1. GA4 with events: `call_click`, `estimate_start`, `estimate_step_2/3/4`, `estimate_submit`, `guide_download`.
2. Google Ads + Meta conversion on `/thank-you.html`.
3. A call-tracking number on paid pages only — never on the pages Google indexes, or NAP consistency breaks and local rankings suffer.
4. Review the form step-drop-off monthly; the step that leaks is the step to fix.

## 9. ⚠ Confirm before this site goes live

These are business commitments, not design decisions. **The build ships with reasonable defaults that the owner must confirm or change.** They are flagged inline in the HTML with `<!-- VERIFY -->` comments and listed in `CONTENT-CHECKLIST.md`.

1. **Warranty length.** Placeholder says a written workmanship warranty. Confirm the term and exact wording.
2. **$1,000 off terms.** Minimum job size? Expiry date? Combinable with other offers?
3. **Free color rendering.** Confirm this is deliverable on every estimate, or scope it to full exteriors only.
4. **Stats** — homes painted, years in business, review count and average must be real numbers.
5. **Testimonials** — every review card ships as a clearly marked placeholder. Replace with verbatim Google reviews before launch. Do not publish invented reviews.
6. **Insurance / licensing** — confirm carrier, coverage and any PA HIC registration number for the footer.
7. **Service radius** — confirm the neighborhood list is where crews actually travel.
8. **Photos** — placeholder images ship at the correct filenames and dimensions; drop the real job photos in over them (see `assets/img/README.md`).
9. **Form endpoint** — the form posts nowhere until an endpoint is set. See `README.md`.
10. **SMS consent language** on the form must match the carrier compliance rules if texting leads.
