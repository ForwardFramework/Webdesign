Meet Brian

The owner, on your job site

Brian started this company because he was tired of watching homeowners get burned by the same three things: a bid that leaves out the prep, a crew that changes every other day, and a final invoice that doesn’t match the quote.

So this business runs the opposite way. The prep spec is printed on your estimate. The same crew works your property start to finish. The quote is the price. And you don’t pay the balance until you’ve walked the finished job with us and signed off on it.

We work in these neighborhoods because we live in them. Painting a house on a street where somebody might recognise the truck at the grocery store is a very effective quality control system.
