Straight answers

The questions everyone asks

For a typical Pittsburgh two‑story, a full exterior repaint generally lands somewhere between $4,500 and $12,000 depending on square footage, substrate, story height, how much prep and carpentry the house needs, and how many colors are involved. Interiors usually run $450–$900 per room for walls, ceiling and trim. Those are ranges, not quotes — the walk‑through is how you get a real number, and it’s free.

Most exteriors run 4 to 8 working days with a full crew, plus weather. Pittsburgh springs and falls being what they are, we build slack into the schedule and tell you the moment a day slips — you shouldn’t have to call and ask where we are.

Yes — fully insured, and we’ll hand you the certificate before we start without you having to ask twice. If any contractor hesitates on that question, that is your answer.

Painted brick is one of the highest‑impact things you can do to a Pittsburgh house — but only with the right system. Brick has to breathe, which means a masonry‑specific mineral or elastomeric‑free breathable coating over properly cleaned, repointed and dry substrate. Done wrong it traps moisture and spalls in three winters. We’ll tell you honestly at the walk‑through whether your brick is a good candidate.

Exteriors want surface temps consistently above 50°F and dry substrate, which in Western PA realistically means late April through late October. August through October is the sweet spot — lower humidity, fewer washouts. Interiors, cabinets and basements we do year‑round, which is why winter is the smart time to book those.
