Local knowledge

Why a Pittsburgh painter matters

This is not a generic climate and it is not generic housing stock. Both change what should go on your house.

{{icon:drop}}

Freeze–thaw is the enemy

Water gets into an open joint, freezes, expands and lifts the coating off. Which is why we caulk obsessively and why a skipped caulking step costs you the whole job.

{{icon:layers}}

A city built out of brick

Painting brick is high‑impact and high‑risk. It has to be repointed, dry and coated with a breathable system, or it spalls within a few winters.

{{icon:hammer}}

Century-old plaster and trim

Settling cracks, lath, lead‑era substrates and trim profiles you can’t buy off the shelf. Handled differently from new drywall.

{{icon:home}}

Hillside houses

Half of Pittsburgh sits on a slope. Staging a three‑story elevation off a hillside is a skill and a safety issue, not an afterthought.

{{icon:cal}}

A short exterior season

Late April to late October, realistically. Knowing when to stop is as important as knowing how to start.

{{icon:leaf}}

Trees, pollen and mildew

North elevations under mature trees grow mildew fast. That gets treated during the wash, not painted over.
