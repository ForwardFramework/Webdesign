How we work inside your house

You should barely notice we’re here

The complaint homeowners have about interior painters is almost never the paint. It’s the mess, the dust, and the not knowing when anyone is showing up.

  • Protect first

    Floors covered wall to wall, furniture moved to the center and wrapped, fixtures and hardware removed rather than taped around.

  • Repair and prep

    Every crack, nail pop and dent filled, sanded and spot‑primed. Sanding is vacuum‑assisted so the dust doesn’t end up in your kitchen.

  • Cut and coat

    Lines cut by hand, two coats rolled, sheen consistent across the whole wall. Trim and doors sprayed where practical.

  • Reset the room

    Hardware back on, furniture back in place, floors vacuumed. Every day, not just on the last day.
