The process

Why sprayed beats brushed, every time

A brushed cabinet door shows every stroke and stays soft for months. A properly sprayed one cures hard and wipes clean like a factory finish. The difference is entirely in the prep and the equipment.

  • Label and remove

    Every door and drawer front numbered and mapped so it goes back exactly where it came from. Hardware bagged by location.

  • Degrease properly

    Twenty years of cooking oil sits in a film on kitchen cabinets. It gets cleaned off with a proper degreaser — skip this and the finish will not bond, full stop.

  • Sand and de‑gloss

    Every face scuffed to break the existing sheen, grain filled where you want a fully smooth modern look, then vacuumed and tacked.

  • 04

    Bond primer

    A dedicated adhesion primer that also blocks tannin bleed from oak and cherry — the reason cheap cabinet jobs go yellow within a year.

  • 05

    Spray the finish coats

    Sprayed in a controlled setup, sanded between coats. Boxes and frames sprayed in place with the kitchen fully masked off.

  • 06

    Cure, rehang, adjust

    Doors go back on new or existing hardware and every hinge is adjusted so the gaps line up. Full cure takes a couple of weeks — we’ll tell you how to treat them meanwhile.
