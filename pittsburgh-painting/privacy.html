Privacy Policy

Last updated .

Who we are

Pittsburgh Painting & Property Solutions is a painting and property renovation contractor serving Greater Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. You can reach us at (412) 537-4866.

What we collect

When you submit an estimate request or contact form we collect the information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, property address and whatever you tell us about the project. We do not ask for and do not want payment card details, Social Security numbers or any other sensitive personal information through this website.

We also collect standard, non‑identifying analytics about how the site is used — pages viewed, approximate region, device type and referring source — to understand which pages are useful and which are not.

How we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, prepare and send your estimate, schedule and carry out work, and follow up about that work. If you tick the SMS consent box we may text you updates about your estimate and job; you can reply STOP at any time to end that.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not pass it to lead‑generation networks or other contractors. The only third parties who ever see it are the service providers we use to run the business — for example email hosting, our estimating and scheduling software, and website analytics — and only to the extent needed to provide those services to us.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and, if we are running ads, for advertising measurement. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and job records for as long as we need them to service the work, honor the workmanship warranty, and meet our tax and legal obligations.

Your choices

Ask us at any time what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call (412) 537-4866 and we’ll take care of it. To stop texts, reply STOP. To stop emails, use the unsubscribe link or just tell us.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
