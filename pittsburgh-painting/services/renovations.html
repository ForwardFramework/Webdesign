Basements & garages

Old Pittsburgh stone, made usable

Half the housing stock in this city has a hundred‑year‑old stone or block basement that the owners treat as a write‑off. Cleaned, sealed and coated properly, it’s the cheapest square footage you will ever add to your house.

  • Find the water first

    Coating a wall that’s actively wicking water is a waste of money. We identify the source — grading, gutters, a failed joint — before anything gets painted.

  • Clean, seal, coat

    Efflorescence and loose material removed, masonry sealer applied, then a breathable masonry coating that brightens the whole space.

  • Floors that take real use

    Epoxy or concrete coatings that stand up to bikes, tools, a workshop or a gym — not the roll‑on kit that lifts in a year.

  • Lighting and finish

    Exposed joists painted out, LED strips run, and suddenly it’s a room instead of storage.
