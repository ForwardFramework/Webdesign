Why fall

Late‑season paint outlasts summer paint

  • Humidity drops

    Coatings cure to their rated hardness instead of skinning over damp. That is the difference between a finish that lasts eight years and one that lasts four.

  • Fewer washouts

    Pittsburgh Augusts and Septembers give us far more workable days than a wet June. Your job actually finishes on schedule.

  • You beat the freeze

    Fresh caulk and paint sealed up before the first freeze–thaw cycle means water stays out of your trim all winter.

  • The calendar is the real deadline

    Once surface temps drop below 50°F we stop exterior work until spring. That’s the only urgency here, and it’s a real one.
