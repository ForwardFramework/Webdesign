Our care promise

Cleaned carefully — and correctly

Car seats are safety equipment. How they’re cleaned matters as much as how clean they get, so we work by the book — your seat’s own manual.

  • By the manufacturer’s instructions

    We look up your model and follow its cleaning guidance. Only components the maker approves for cleaning are removed and cleaned.

  • Gentle, family-safe products

    No harsh solvents or bleach on the shell or harness. We don’t claim to “sanitize” or “disinfect” a harness — that can damage webbing and most manufacturers advise against it.

  • Dried, not rushed

    Seats are air-dried thoroughly before reassembly. You never get a damp seat back, and we never use heat that can weaken components.

  • Reassembled properly

    Covers and harnesses go back exactly as they came off, routed per the manual. We’ll flag anything we notice — an expired seat, a missing part — so you can act on it.

Good questions

Frequently asked

How long will my car seat be gone?
Most seats are picked up and delivered back within 24–48 hours. Drying is the part we won’t rush — a properly dried seat is worth the extra few hours. If you have a second seat or a loaner, we’ll work around your schedule.
Do I need to take the seat out of the car?
Yes — please uninstall the seat before pickup and leave it somewhere easy to grab. Installing a car seat is specific to your vehicle and your seat’s manual, so we return it to you rather than reinstalling it.
What if the seat is really, really bad?
We’ve seen it. Blowouts, car sickness, a smoothie that lived in the cup holder for a month. Heavy soil adds $10–$20, and we’ll always tell you before we start — never after.
Do you sanitize or disinfect the harness?
We clean harness straps only in the way your seat’s manufacturer permits. We don’t make sanitizing or disinfecting claims about harness webbing, because the chemicals and methods that do that can weaken it. Safety first, every time.
Do you clean strollers and boosters too?
We do. Boosters are $40 and strollers are $55–$65 depending on size and condition (single or double). Add either to a car seat booking and it goes out on the same pickup.
How many seats does a group booking need?
Four seats booked for the same date and location unlock the group rate — and they don’t all have to belong to one family. Four moms with one seat each counts. See groups & events for the full rate card.
Do you travel outside your service area?
Often, yes — there’s a $10–$20 travel fee depending on distance, quoted when you book. For group days a little farther out, the travel fee is usually waived.
How do I pay?
Payment is due at delivery, and we take the usual digital options. Group hosts can either collect from the group ahead of time or have each family pay their own seat.
