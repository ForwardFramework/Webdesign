About

About Osas Construction Group

A Pittsburgh general contractor built around a simple idea: fewer phone calls, clearer pricing, work that holds up.

Who is Osas Construction Group?

Osas Construction Group is a licensed and insured general contractor serving Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and the surrounding counties. The company holds Pennsylvania Home Improvement Contractor registration PA200902 and general contractor license GC-2025-006171, and works on residential and commercial property across interior remodeling, exterior replacement, concrete and outdoor construction, hauling and seasonal maintenance.

What we are

Osas Construction Group is a general contractor, a home improvement contractor and a commercial contractor — the three lines printed on our card, and an accurate description of the work. We renovate houses, we replace roofs and siding, we pour concrete and build decks, we landscape and haul and plow.

Most of our projects touch more than one of those. A bathroom remodel finds a roof leak. A deck build needs the retaining wall rebuilt first. A cleanout turns into a demolition. Keeping those trades under one contract is the whole point.

How we price

Itemized, in writing, before anything is demolished. Labor, materials and disposal broken out so you can see the cost of each piece and make real decisions about scope — keep it, cut it, phase it to next year.

No hidden fees, no transparent-pricing slogan that evaporates at invoice time. If something has to change mid-project — rotted subfloor, a joist that needs sistering, sheathing behind the siding that is gone — you see it, you get a price for it, and you approve it before we continue.

How we work in your house

Floor protection through occupied space. Dust containment at the work area. Debris in our trailers rather than your driveway. A crew that cleans up at the end of the day instead of at the end of the job.

It sounds like table stakes. Anyone who has lived through a renovation knows it is not.

Licensing and insurance

Pennsylvania requires home improvement contractors to register with the Attorney General's office and to put residential contracts in writing. We are registered as PA HIC #PA200902 and carry general contractor license GC-2025-006171.

We are fully insured for residential and commercial work, and a certificate of insurance is available on request — property managers and commercial clients usually want one on file before work starts, and that is a reasonable thing to ask any contractor for.

Process

How a project runs

Four steps from the first call to the final walkthrough.

  1. Call or text

    Reach us at (412) 923-2092 or send the estimate form. Tell us the address, the scope and your timeline.

  2. On-site walkthrough

    We look at the actual conditions — structure, access, drainage, existing finishes — instead of guessing from photos.

  3. Written, itemized quote

    Line-item pricing for labor, materials and disposal. What you approve is what you pay.

  4. Build and clean up

    Scheduled crews, permits pulled where required, daily cleanup and a final walkthrough before we invoice.
