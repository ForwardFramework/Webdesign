Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about how we quote, schedule, permit and finish work — plus what we will not pretend to know until we have seen your property.

Osas Construction Group, in brief

Common questions about Osas Construction Group: the company is a licensed (PA HIC #PA200902, GC-2025-006171) and insured general contractor in Pittsburgh, PA. Estimates are free and done on site, contracts and pricing are itemized in writing, and the company handles permits where the municipality requires them.

Licensing, insurance and paperwork

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Pennsylvania Home Improvement Contractor registration PA200902 and general contractor license GC-2025-006171, and we are fully insured. Certificates of insurance are available on request, which is standard for commercial clients and property managers.

Do I get a written contract?

Always. Pennsylvania's Home Improvement Consumer Protection Act requires written contracts for residential home improvement work, and we itemize scope, materials, labor and disposal so nothing is hiding in a lump sum.

Who pulls the permits?

We do, for work that requires them. Requirements vary — the City of Pittsburgh permits through PLI, and each surrounding municipality has its own code office. We confirm for your address.

Estimates, pricing and payment

Are estimates free?

Yes, and we do them on site. A phone quote for construction work is a guess, and guesses turn into change orders.

Why won't you give me a price over the phone?

For simple, repeatable services — a dumpster rental, a gutter cleaning — we often can. For construction we cannot responsibly, because the cost depends on access, conditions, substrate and what is behind the wall.

What happens if something unexpected turns up mid-project?

We stop, show you what we found, price it as its own line item and get your approval before continuing. Rot, failed framing and hidden water damage are the usual culprits in older Pittsburgh homes.

Do you do small jobs?

Yes — a drywall patch, a single door, a gutter cleaning, a one-time cleanout. Not every call has to be a renovation.

Scheduling and the work itself

How far out are you booking?

It depends on the season and the trade. Hauling, cleanouts and snow move quickly; roofing and full renovations book further out, especially in the busy months. Call and we will give you a real date rather than an optimistic one.

Can I stay in the house during the work?

Usually. We phase work and seal off active areas. Kitchens and single-bathroom homes are the genuinely hard cases, and we will tell you which weeks will be difficult.

Do you clean up?

Daily, and completely at the end. We own our trailers, so debris leaves with us instead of sitting in your driveway.

Do you work in winter?

Yes. Interior work runs all winter, and we plow and salt. Some exterior work — concrete pours, exterior painting, roofing in freezing weather — is genuinely weather-dependent, and we will tell you when waiting is the right call.

Service area and property types

What areas do you cover?

Pittsburgh and the surrounding region: all city neighborhoods, the South Hills, North Hills, eastern suburbs, the airport corridor, and into Washington and Butler counties. If you are not sure, call and ask.

Do you work on commercial property?

Yes — tenant fit-outs, interior renovation, exterior repairs, lot maintenance, pressure washing and seasonal snow contracts, for owners and property managers.

Do you work with landlords and rental properties?

Yes, including turnovers, eviction cleanouts and recurring maintenance across multiple addresses.
