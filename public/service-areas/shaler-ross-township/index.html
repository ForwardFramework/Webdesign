Does Osas Construction Group work in Shaler & Ross Township?

Osas Construction Group serves Shaler Township and Ross Township, PA with roofing, siding, concrete, retaining walls, remodeling and snow removal. North Hills hillside lots with steep driveways and aging retaining walls are routine work here. Call or text (412) 923-2092.

Shaler and Ross are classic North Hills: mid-century homes on hillside lots above the Allegheny, steep driveways, and retaining walls that were built when the plans went in and are now leaning.

Failed walls, cracked driveways on a grade, and drainage running downhill into a basement are the three things we see most often up here — and they are usually the same problem in three forms.

  • Steep driveways

Questions

Shaler & Ross Township — local questions

My retaining wall is leaning — can it be repaired or does it need replacing?

A wall that is tipping or bulging has usually lost the argument with water pressure behind it, and cosmetic repair does not fix that. Replacement with drainage stone, filter fabric and a drain outlet is the durable answer. We will tell you honestly which one you are looking at.

Can you plow a steep driveway?

Yes. Steep drives need salt and timing more than horsepower, which is why a seasonal contract works better than calling after the ice sets.
