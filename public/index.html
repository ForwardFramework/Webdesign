Process

How a project runs

No mystery, no moving target. Four steps from the first call to the final walkthrough.

  1. Call or text

    Reach us at (412) 923-2092 or send the estimate form. Tell us the address, the scope and your timeline.

  2. On-site walkthrough

    We look at the actual conditions — structure, access, drainage, existing finishes — instead of guessing from photos.

  3. Written, itemized quote

    Line-item pricing for labor, materials and disposal. What you approve is what you pay.

  4. Build and clean up

    Scheduled crews, permits pulled where required, daily cleanup and a final walkthrough before we invoice.

Questions

Questions people ask before they call

Is Osas Construction Group licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold Pennsylvania Home Improvement Contractor registration PA200902 and general contractor license GC-2025-006171, and we are fully insured. We are glad to provide a certificate of insurance before work begins.

What areas do you serve?

Pittsburgh and the surrounding region — all city neighborhoods plus the South Hills, North Hills, eastern suburbs and airport corridor, extending into Washington and Butler counties. If you are not sure whether your address is covered, call and ask.

How much does an estimate cost?

Nothing. Estimates are free, and we do them on site rather than over the phone, because what a project actually costs depends on conditions you cannot see in a photograph.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text (412) 923-2092, or send the estimate form on this site. Tell us the property address, what you want done and your timeline, and we will schedule a walkthrough.

Do you do both residential and commercial work?

Yes. Homeowners, landlords, property managers and business owners. Commercial work includes tenant fit-outs, interior renovation, lot maintenance and seasonal snow contracts.

Do you handle permits?

Yes, for work that requires them. Requirements differ between the City of Pittsburgh's PLI department and each surrounding municipality, and we confirm what your specific address needs before work starts.

Can one contractor really do all of this?

That is the model. We keep crews across the interior trades, the exterior trades and the outdoor and hauling work, which is why a kitchen remodel that turns into a roof repair does not become someone else's problem — or a second mobilization fee.
