Good to know

Can I drive a golf cart on Anna Maria Island?

Yes — if it’s street legal. Our whole fleet is, which means lights, signals, mirrors, seat belts and a VIN. Street-legal carts are allowed on roads posted at 35 MPH or less, and a licensed driver aged 18 or over has to be at the wheel.

Do you deliver to my rental on the island?

Yes, and delivery and pickup are free anywhere on Anna Maria Island — Anna Maria, Holmes Beach and Bradenton Beach included. Tell us the address and your arrival time and the cart will be waiting.
