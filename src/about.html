Our story

A family business, not a rental counter

Coastal Custom Carts, LLC is Kelly and Zack — a local family whose purpose and passion is putting people in high quality, street-legal golf carts at prices that are actually fair.

Why we started

We got tired of the fine print

Anyone who has rented on vacation knows the routine: a great headline price, then a processing fee, then a delivery fee, then a pushy upsell at handover.

We built Coastal Custom Carts to be the opposite of that. One honest quote. Free delivery and pickup. A clean, charged, genuinely nice cart waiting when you arrive. And two actual humans on the other end of the phone if anything comes up while you’re here.

That’s the whole business model. It has earned us 95 five-star reviews and a lot of guests who book the same week again next year.

What we stand for

Four promises we don’t break

Free delivery and pickup throughout Manatee & Sarasota County. You never visit a depot.

Beyond rentals

We build carts too

The same carts you rent from us are the ones we customise and sell. Paint, seating, wheels, audio, lighting and street-legal packages, specced the way you want them.

Plenty of our buyers started as renters — they spent a week on the island in one of ours and decided they wanted their own.
