Bradenton Beach questions

Good to know

Can I take the cart over the bridge to Cortez?

Our carts are street legal, so the rule is the same everywhere: roads posted at 35 MPH or less. Check the posted limit on the route you have in mind, and if you’re not sure, call us — we know the island roads well.

How early should I book for Bridge Street weekends?

Peak season, spring break and holiday weekends go first. Earlier is better, but we’ve rescued plenty of last-minute trips — it’s always worth a text.
