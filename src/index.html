Good to know

Questions, answered

Are your golf carts street legal?

Yes. Our entire fleet is street legal, which means headlights, tail lights, turn signals, mirrors, seat belts and a VIN. You can legally drive them on Anna Maria Island’s posted roads and throughout the neighborhoods we serve.

Who is allowed to drive the cart?

Any licensed driver 18 or older who is listed on the rental agreement. Because our carts are street legal, a valid driver’s license is required — the same rules as driving a car apply.

Is delivery really free?

Really free. Delivery and pickup are complimentary throughout Manatee & Sarasota County, including Anna Maria Island, Holmes Beach, Bradenton Beach, Lido Key, Siesta Key and Lakewood Ranch. No processing fees, no hidden fees, no sales gimmicks.

Gas or electric — which should I choose?

Electric carts run on top-of-the-line lithium batteries: quiet, quick and no fuel stops, just plug in overnight at your rental. Gas carts are the pick if you’re covering a lot of miles each day and would rather top up at a pump. Tell us how you’ll use it and we’ll point you to the right one.

Can you accommodate kids?

Absolutely. Booster seats and car seats are available on request at no drama — just tell us how many and what ages when you book.

How much does it cost, and how long can I rent for?

We rent in flat-rate packages rather than by the day. A 4 seater is $350 for 3 days, $400 for 5 days or $450 for a full week; a 6 seater is $450, $500 and $550 for the same lengths. That price is everything — delivery, pickup and the whole cart. Need a different length? Call or text us and we’ll work it out.

How far in advance should I book?

For peak season, spring break and holiday weeks, the earlier the better — those dates go first. That said, we’ve saved plenty of last-minute trips, so it’s always worth a call or text to 941-312-1494.

What happens if something goes wrong with the cart?

Call us and we’ll come out. We’re local, we check in during your stay, and we service everything we rent. You’re never waiting on an out-of-town call center.

Do you rent for long stays or seasonal visits?

Yes. Our published packages run to 7 days, and longer stays come with better rates — the weekly price already works out at about $64 a day for a 4 seater. Snowbirds and seasonal visitors, just call and we’ll quote your dates.

Is there a rental agreement to sign?

Yes, and you can read it before you commit. Our rental agreement is signed online, so everything is squared away before your cart is delivered.
