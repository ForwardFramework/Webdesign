Questions

Answered before you ask

How much does house cleaning cost in Lakewood Ranch?

Most homes in Lakewood Ranch, Bradenton and Sarasota land between $135 and $290 per visit, depending on size and how often we come. Recurring plans are the cheapest way to get cleaned — every-two-weeks service saves 15% off the one-time rate and weekly saves 20%. Use the instant estimator on this page for a number in about ten seconds, or ask for a firm flat quote and we'll hold that price.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. There is no contract and no cancellation fee. Your recurring discount is applied visit by visit — keep it as long as you like the service, and pause or stop any time with 48 hours' notice.

Are you insured and are your cleaners background-checked?

Yes to both. Yasse's Cleaning carries general liability insurance, and every cleaner is background-checked, personally trained by us and covered while working in your home. We're happy to send a certificate of insurance to your HOA or property manager.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Most of our clients aren't. You can leave a key, a lockbox code or a garage code, and we'll lock up when we're done. Seasonal residents get a photo of each finished room after the visit so you can see your home even when you're up north.

What's the difference between a standard clean and a deep clean?

A standard clean maintains an already-maintained home: kitchens, bathrooms, dusting, vacuuming, mopping, beds made. A deep clean is the reset — baseboards, door frames, window tracks, ceiling fans, grout, buildup behind and under things. First-time homes usually need a deep clean, and then a standard plan holds it there.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Everything comes with us — vacuums, microfiber, mops and products. Our default line is eco-friendly and safe around kids and pets. If you'd prefer we use your own products or a specific disinfectant, just leave them out and tell us.
