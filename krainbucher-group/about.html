About

Brian Krainbucher is the President and CEO of The Krainbucher Group, where he works directly with every client the firm takes on.

The practice was built around a specific frustration: financial products are usually sold before anyone has properly established what the family is actually exposed to. A policy arrives, a contract is signed, and nobody ever puts a number on the scenario the household could not survive.

So the order of work here is deliberately inverted. We map the exposures first — the lost income, the mortgage, the diagnosis at 54, the surviving spouse’s tax bill — and only then look at which of them are worth transferring to an insurance company. Some of them are not, and saying so is part of the job.

Because the agency is independent, there is no house product to defend. That matters most in underwriting, where two carriers can price the same medical history very differently, and in retirement income, where the right contract depends far more on your timeline than on anyone’s shelf.

How we operate

Four commitments we will hold ourselves to.

01

The number before the product

You will see the size of the gap in writing before you hear a single product recommendation. If the gap is small enough to self-insure, we will say so.

02

Trade-offs stated out loud

Every contract gives something up. Surrender periods, caps, rider costs and conversion deadlines get explained before you sign, not discovered in year three.

03

Independent by design

Multiple carriers, no quota on any one of them. The recommendation follows your underwriting and your timeline.

04

We answer the phone

You deal with the person who wrote your policy — at application, at annual review, and on the day your family has to make a claim.

Licensing

Licensed life and health insurance producer. Products are offered through appointed carriers; availability, features and riders vary by state and by carrier.

What we don’t do

We do not draft legal documents, prepare tax returns or manage securities portfolios. We work alongside your attorney and CPA and will tell you plainly when a question belongs to one of them.

Reviews

Policies are not a set-and-forget purchase. Income, health, mortgages and beneficiaries change — an annual review is included for every client, at no cost.
