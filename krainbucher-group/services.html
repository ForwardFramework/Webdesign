Service 04

Mortgage Protection

A term policy sized and dated to the debt on your home, so that if the earner dies during the mortgage, the balance is cleared and the family keeps the house rather than the payment.

It is the simplest coverage we write, and the one most often bought badly — usually because a letter arrived from a lender offering a policy that pays the bank rather than your spouse.

What we look at

  • Remaining balance and years left, not the original loan amount.
  • Whether the benefit should be level or decline with the balance.
  • Who receives the money — your beneficiary, not the lender, keeps the choice to pay off or invest.
  • Whether one policy covering income replacement makes the separate mortgage policy unnecessary.
The honest trade-off

Lender-sold mortgage protection is convenient and almost always more expensive per dollar of benefit than an individually underwritten term policy, and it pays the bank. A decreasing-benefit policy is cheaper but leaves nothing once the loan is retired.

Who this is for

Anyone inside the first fifteen years of a mortgage with a household that could not carry the payment on one income. Coverage is cheapest the day you are healthiest, which is today.

Service 05

Living Benefits

Policies that give you access to your benefits without paying a penalty for using them. A qualifying critical, chronic or terminal illness can let you accelerate part of the death benefit while you are alive — when the money is needed for treatment, a mortgage, or simply to stop working.

Statistically, a working-age adult is far more likely to face a disabling illness than to die during their working years. Coverage that only pays at death answers the less likely event.

Typically triggered by

  • Critical illness — heart attack, stroke, or a covered cancer diagnosis.
  • Chronic illness — inability to perform a set number of daily living activities.
  • Terminal illness — a prognosis inside the carrier’s defined window.
The honest trade-off

Accelerating a benefit reduces what is paid at death, riders are subject to eligibility and underwriting, definitions differ meaningfully between carriers, and some riders carry an explicit charge. The rider wording is the product — we read it with you before you sign.

Service 06

Legacy Planning

Making it easier to leave something behind for the people you choose — structured so it arrives quickly, privately, and without forcing anyone to sell an asset at the wrong time to cover a bill.

A life insurance death benefit is generally income-tax-free to the beneficiary and passes outside of probate by contract. That combination is why it remains the most efficient way to hand a specific number to a specific person on a specific day.

Where we usually start

  • Beneficiary designations — the single most common and most expensive oversight we find.
  • Liquidity for final expenses, estate settlement costs and the tax due on inherited retirement accounts.
  • Equalising an inheritance when one child will receive the business or the property and the others will not.
  • Coordinating with the attorney and CPA already in your corner, rather than around them.
Please note

We are not attorneys or accountants and do not draft documents or give tax advice. We build the funding side and work alongside the professionals who handle the rest.
