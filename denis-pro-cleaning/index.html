Questions

Everything you're wondering, answered

How much does house cleaning cost in Pittsburgh?

Most standard cleans in the Pittsburgh area run $135–$255 per visit depending on the size of your home, and a one-time deep clean typically falls between $210 and $400. Recurring plans lower the per-visit price by 10–20% because there's less build-up each time. Use the instant quote at the top of this page for a price on your exact home — it takes about 20 seconds.

Are you insured, bonded and background-checked?

Yes on all three. Denis Pro Cleaning Services carries general liability insurance and a bond, and every cleaner passes a criminal background check and hands-on training before they set foot in a client's home. We're happy to send our certificate of insurance before your first visit.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all — most of our recurring clients aren't. You can leave a key, a lockbox code, a garage code, or let us in and head out. We text when we arrive and when we finish, and we lock up exactly the way you asked.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. Vacuums, mops, microfiber cloths and cleaning products are all included in the price — nothing extra to buy. We use pet- and kid-safe products by default, and we'll happily use eco-friendly or fragrance-free lines, or your own products, if you prefer. Just tell us when you book.

What's the difference between a standard clean and a deep clean?

A standard clean covers everything on the room-by-room checklist — surfaces, kitchen, bathrooms, floors, dusting and high-touch points. A deep clean adds the build-up work: baseboards, blinds, window sills, door frames, grout, vents, light fixtures, inside appliances and behind furniture. We recommend starting with a deep clean, then keeping it up with a weekly, bi-weekly or monthly package.

Am I locked into a contract?

Never. Recurring packages are month-to-month. Skip a visit, pause for a vacation, switch from weekly to monthly, or stop entirely — just give us 24 hours' notice and there's no fee either way.

What if I'm not happy with the cleaning?

Tell us within 24 hours and we come back and re-clean the areas that missed the mark, free. That's our 100% Shine Guarantee, and there's no form to fill out — a text is enough.

How soon can you come out?

We usually have same-week openings, and often next-day for one-time and move-out cleans. Pick a date on the booking calendar above to see what's currently available, or call 203-627-9940 for the fastest answer.

How do I pay?

You pay after the job is done, never before. We accept all major credit and debit cards, Zelle, Venmo, cash and check. Recurring clients can keep a card securely on file so each visit is charged automatically after it's completed.
