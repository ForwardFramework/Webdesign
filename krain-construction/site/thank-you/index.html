What happens next

Four steps, and none of them cost you anything.

1

We call you back

Today, if you sent this during business hours. We will ask a few questions about the project so the site visit is not wasted time.

2

We walk your property

At a time that works for you. Measurements, photos, and an honest read on slope, access, utilities and structure.

3

Written line-item proposal

Real numbers you can read and compare. No mystery allowances designed to become change orders later.

4

You decide

No follow-up pressure, no expiring discounts. If it is not the right time, we will still be here when it is.

While you wait

Three things worth five minutes.

  • Take photos of the areaWide shots plus close-ups of anything that concerns you. Text them to us and we can often answer half your questions before we arrive.
  • Find your plot plan or surveyIf you have one, it saves time on setbacks and makes the permit application straightforward.
  • Write down your must-haves and nice-to-havesKnowing which is which is how we keep a project inside a budget instead of over it.
