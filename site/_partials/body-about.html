About Rough Diamond

A Pittsburgh pressure washing company built on one idea: almost every filthy surface is a good-looking surface that nobody has cleaned properly yet.

Our Story

The Name Is The Whole Idea

A rough diamond is worth exactly what a polished one is worth. It just hasn't been cleaned up yet. That is every driveway, deck and storefront we pull up to.

Pittsburgh weather is hard on exteriors. Freeze-thaw cycles work salt and grit into concrete. Humid summers grow algae on every north-facing surface. Add a few winters of it and people stop seeing the dirt — it just becomes what the driveway looks like now.

Then we clean a strip of it, and the reaction is always the same. That is the part of the job we like.
