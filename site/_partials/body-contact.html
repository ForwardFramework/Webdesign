Common Questions

Good To Know

How much does pressure washing cost?
It depends on square footage, the surface and how bad the buildup is — a small front walk and a 1,200 square foot driveway are very different jobs. That's why every quote is specific to your property. Send a photo or two and we can usually give you a number the same day.
Do you need to use my water?
Yes, in most cases we hook up to an outside spigot. It uses less water than you'd think — a surface cleaner is far more efficient than running a garden hose at the same job for an hour. If your property doesn't have a working outside tap, tell us when you book and we'll plan around it.
Will pressure washing damage my siding, deck or roof?
Not when the method matches the surface. Concrete can take real pressure. Siding, roofs and older wood cannot — those get soft washed with detergent at low pressure instead. Damage almost always comes from someone using driveway settings on a surface that couldn't take it.
Do I need to be home?
Not necessarily. As long as we have access to the areas being cleaned and an outside water spigot, we can work while you're out. We'd rather you were there at the end to walk the job with us, but we can also send photos when we finish.
How long does it take?
Most residential driveways run one to three hours. A full house soft wash is usually half a day. Whole-exterior packages are typically a single full day. We'll give you a realistic window when we quote it.
How often should this be done?
Around Pittsburgh, most homes look best on a yearly cycle — concrete and siding pick up algae fast in our humidity. North-facing surfaces and heavily shaded properties sometimes want it twice a year. Roofs are more like every two to four years.
What about my plants and landscaping?
Beds get pre-wet before we start and rinsed again when we finish, which dilutes anything that drifts. Anything especially delicate, point it out to us and we'll cover it or work around it.
What if it rains?
Light rain is not a problem — we're already applying a lot of water. Heavy storms or lightning mean we reschedule, and you'll hear from us that morning rather than being left waiting.
