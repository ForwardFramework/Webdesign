Our Services

Six core services covering just about every exterior surface on a Pittsburgh property. Every one of them starts with a free quote and a straight number.

Service 01

Driveways & Concrete

Concrete is porous, so everything that lands on it soaks in — road salt, oil drips, leaf tannins, algae. A garden hose moves it around. A surface cleaner takes it off.

  • Driveways & Garage ApronsEven, overlap-free passes so you don't end up with zebra striping.
  • Sidewalks & WalkwaysIncluding the slick green film that turns a front walk into a hazard.
  • Stain TreatmentOil, rust and battery acid spots get pre-treated before the wash.
  • Steps, Curbs & EdgesHand-detailed with a wand where the surface cleaner can't reach.

Service 02

House Soft Washing

High pressure on siding is how you get water behind the panels and streaks in the finish. Soft washing does the work with detergent and dwell time instead of force.

  • All Common SidingsVinyl, aluminum, wood, brick, stone and stucco.
  • Kills It, Doesn't Just Move ItTreating the organic growth means it stays gone far longer than a rinse.
  • Soffits, Fascia & Gutter FacesThose black tiger stripes on the gutters come off too.
  • Landscaping ProtectedBeds get pre-wet and rinsed down on the way out.
Quote My House

Service 03

Decks & Fences

Wood is the easiest surface to wreck with a pressure washer. Too close, too much pressure, wrong angle and you've raised the grain permanently. We work with the board, not against it.

  • Pressure-Treated, Cedar & CompositeEach one gets a different setting and a different approach.
  • Railings, Spindles & StairsThe fiddly parts are included, not upcharged.
  • Stain-Ready FinishIf you're refinishing, we leave it clean and open so the stain takes evenly.
  • Vinyl & Aluminum FencingGreen algae on the shady side comes off without scrubbing.
Quote My Deck

Service 04

Roof Soft Washing

Those dark streaks running down your shingles are a living algae colony feeding on the limestone filler. Pressure washing a roof strips granules and shortens its life. We don't do that.

  • Low-Pressure, Chemistry-FirstThe treatment does the work; the rinse just carries it away.
  • Asphalt Shingle SafeGranules stay bedded where the manufacturer put them.
  • Gutters & DownspoutsExterior faces brightened while we're up there.
  • Curb Appeal, FastNothing ages a house from the street faster than a streaked roof.
Quote My Roof

Service 05

Patios & Pavers

Most people forget what color their pavers actually are. Under the moss and the gray film there's usually a warm terracotta or a clean bluestone waiting.

  • Pavers, Flagstone & BluestonePressure tuned so joints stay put and stone doesn't pit.
  • Stamped & Colored ConcreteHandled carefully so the color coat and sealer survive.
  • Weeds, Moss & Joint GrowthCleared out of the joints instead of trimmed at the top.
  • Optional Re-SandingPolymeric sand back in the joints to lock everything down.
