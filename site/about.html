The Approach

Built Around Your Downtime, Not Our Schedule

Contractors and equipment owners across Western Pennsylvania lose money the moment a machine stops. The traditional answer — load it, haul it, wait in line at a dealer — can turn a half-day repair into a week off the job.

Mobile service removes most of that. The truck comes to the machine with diagnostic equipment, hydraulic tooling and a welder aboard, so the majority of failures get found and fixed in a single visit.

You deal with the same person the whole way through: the one who answered the phone, diagnosed the fault and turned the wrenches.

  • The Shop Comes To YouNo loading, no lowboy, no tow bill. The service truck rolls to your jobsite, shop or yard with the tools and common parts already aboard.
  • Answered 24/7Machines break at 5am and on Sundays. The phone gets answered around the clock, and emergency calls get moved to the front.
  • One Mechanic, Start To FinishStephen Kotouch does the diagnosis and the repair himself. Nothing gets lost handing the job between a service writer and a tech.
  • Straight AnswersYou hear what failed, what it takes to fix it and what it costs before the work starts. No surprise line items at the end.
