Your roof and your siding are one weather system. Water that gets past a wall detail ends up in exactly the same place as water that gets past a flashing detail.

Questions

Siding — Common Questions

Can you match my existing siding?

Often, yes — though older siding may have faded or been discontinued. We tell you honestly before we start whether a repair will blend in or whether replacing a full elevation will look better.

Is siding damage covered by insurance?

Wind and hail damage to siding is commonly covered under the same policy as your roof. We document siding alongside roof damage so the claim reflects the whole loss rather than half of it.

Can you do siding and roofing at the same time?

Yes, and it is usually the better approach. Roof, gutters, soffit, fascia and siding meet at details that leak when two different contractors each stop at their own edge.
