Roof Repair & Leak Repair in Pittsburgh

Where water shows up on your ceiling is almost never where it entered the roof. We trace it back to the actual entry point, so the repair holds instead of moving the leak six feet down the rafter.

Top Gun Roofing repairs roof leaks throughout the Pittsburgh metro by locating the true source of the water rather than patching the stain below it. Common causes we correct include failed step flashing at chimneys and walls, split valleys, cracked pipe boots, wind-lifted shingles and ice-dam damage. Roof Repair — Common Questions

Do you take small roof repair jobs?

Yes. We regularly handle single-flashing repairs, cracked pipe boots, and soffit and fascia work. Our customers consistently note that we treat small jobs with the same respect as full replacements.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

It depends on the age of the roof, how widespread the damage is, and the condition of the decking. If your roof has ten good years left and one bad valley, you need a repair. We will tell you honestly which one you are looking at, with photos to back it up.

Can you find a leak if the ceiling stain is not under the damage?

Yes, and this is normal. Water travels along rafters and decking before it drops through drywall, so the stain is often several feet from the entry point. We trace it back to the source rather than patching the nearest shingle.

My roof is only a few years old and it is leaking. Is that possible?

Absolutely, and it usually means an installation error rather than material failure — improperly layered flashing, missing ice-and-water shield, or two different roofing systems joined incorrectly. We find that kind of mistake regularly.
