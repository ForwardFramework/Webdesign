    Straight answers

    Questions people ask us first

    Is the online estimate an actual quote?

    No — and we’d rather be upfront about that than surprise you later. The estimator gives you a planning range built from current Southwest Florida installed pricing for the product, size and options you picked. Your real price depends on the actual measurements, how your walls and roof are built, your wind zone, your county’s permit fees and the exact products you choose. The written, itemized quote comes after a free on-site measurement, and it holds for 30 days.

    Do I need a permit?

    Permanent aluminum structures and hurricane-rated screen systems need a county permit and sealed engineering drawings in Florida. Retractable awnings depend on the jurisdiction and how they’re attached. We handle the drawings, the submittal and the inspections as part of the job — you don’t deal with the county.

    How much cooler does shade actually make it?

    Shade products can lower the ambient temperature by around 15° on hot sunny days. Just as importantly, blocking the sun before it hits your glass keeps the heat out of the house, which takes load off your air conditioning. Exactly how much you feel depends on your exposure, the fabric you choose and how much of the opening you cover.

    Are the roll screens really hurricane rated?

    The hurricane-rated systems we install carry Florida Product Approval and are tested to a specific design pressure. The rating that matters is the one for your opening size and wind zone — a product approved for a small window isn’t automatically approved for a 20-foot lanai bay. Ask your designer for the approval number covering your specific openings, and we’ll put it in writing on the quote.

    How long does it take?

    Timelines are driven by three things: product lead time from the manufacturer, county permit turnaround and our install schedule. Motorised and hurricane-rated products with engineering take meaningfully longer than a simple retractable awning. Your designer will give you a realistic date range in writing rather than a number designed to win the sale.

    What’s covered by warranty?

    Every product carries its manufacturer’s warranty — these differ by fabric, frame and motor — and our workmanship warranty covers the installation itself. The exact terms for the products you pick are written into your quote. Have your designer walk you through them before you sign anything; a good warranty conversation is a sign you’re dealing with the right company.

    Do you offer financing?

    Yes, through third-party lending partners, subject to credit approval. Terms and rates vary by applicant. The monthly figure shown in the estimator is an illustration to help you plan, not an offer of credit — your designer can walk you through the real options.
