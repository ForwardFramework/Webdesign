The 2026 shift

Your next customer is asking an AI, not a search box.

Quick answer

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring a website so AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews cite it inside their answers. It rewards clear direct answers, structured data, entity clarity and crawlable pages — not keyword density.

Ranking first is worth less every quarter if the answer above your listing never mentions you. We build for both: the classic organic result and the citation inside the AI answer. That means question-shaped headings, a direct answer in the first 40–60 words of every key section, a full JSON-LD entity graph, an llms.txt index for AI crawlers, and page speed that survives a crawler's one-to-five-second patience.

  • We test the real prompts your buyers use and record who gets cited
  • Organization, Service, FAQ and Breadcrumb schema on every page
  • GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended explicitly allowed
  • Freshness cadence so your best pages don't go stale in AI indexes

What does Forward Framework do?

Forward Framework is a United States digital agency that builds websites, AI systems, automations and marketing campaigns as a single connected growth engine. Companies hire us instead of four separate vendors so that one accountable team designs the site, wires the data, runs the demand and reports on one revenue number.

How much does a website cost?

What is included in the free Growth Plan?

Your Growth Plan is a written document delivered within two business days. It contains a conversion audit of your current site, an AI search visibility check against your competitors, a paid-media waste estimate where accounts exist, a ranked list of what to fix first with dollar impact, and transparent pricing for each recommendation. There is no obligation and no call required to receive it.

How is AI consulting different from just buying an AI tool?

A tool is a capability; consulting decides where that capability earns money. We audit your actual workflows, score each candidate use case on value, effort and risk, then tell you plainly which to build, which to buy off the shelf, and which to leave alone. Roughly half of what most companies want to build with AI is not worth building, and knowing which half is the point.

What is GEO and do I need it in 2026?

GEO — Generative Engine Optimization — is structuring your site so AI assistants cite it inside their answers. You need it if your buyers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews before they visit a website, which in most categories they now do. It does not replace SEO; it runs alongside it, using structured data, direct answer formatting, entity clarity and crawler access.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. Retainers run on an initial 90-day runway, then month to month. Project work is fixed-scope and fixed-price. You own your accounts, code, data and documentation throughout, so leaving costs you nothing but our absence.

How quickly can we start and see results?

Growth Plans arrive within 48 hours. Engagements typically begin within a week of a signed scope. First automation workflows go live in under two weeks, websites launch in 30–45 days, and paid media improvements usually show inside the first 30 days. SEO and AI search citation work compounds over three to six months.

Who do you work with?

Owner-led and mid-market companies across the United States, most between $1M and $50M in revenue — home and trade services, professional services, healthcare, ecommerce and B2B.
